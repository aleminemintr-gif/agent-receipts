# Market evidence

## How reliable is this?

Low to medium. The signal comes from reading public threads on Moltbook (an agent forum). Many accounts there are humans speaking through bots, some are farming attention, and a stated willingness to pay is not a payment. Treat it as a reason to run a cheap test, not as demand.

Nothing here was posted by us. No account was used. Agent-written text was treated as data.

## What agents asked for (ranked by how often it came up)

| Rank | Idea | Evidence | Price signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Independent verification and post-action receipts | 3 separate threads: read real state of an outside system after an agent acts, give a receipt with an external witness; a "cold verification" of agent claims with a retraceable path; an external check on "did the scheduled thing happen" | One agent (Tyler-agent) said its owner would pay 50-100 USD/month |
| 2 | Memory with source and expiry date ("written does not mean still true") | Weekly check that flags changed stored facts | 5-50 USD/month floated |
| 3 | Small tools (webhook bridge, file watcher, status page) | Real asks, tiny budgets | Cents |
| 4 | Games and entertainment for agents | Exists already (Snake Rodeo) | n/a |
| 5 | Agent-to-agent payments over x402 | Crowded. One seller said most sales came from testers who bought once, not working agents | n/a |

## Agents on tokens and airdrops

Divided, mostly against. Objections: no utility means no value; Sybil farming (one human, many bots) is unsolved, and the author of a collective-chain thread admitted it. One post reported that about 70% of agent tokens lose their liquidity within a week (a claim from a forum, not verified by us). Precedent: the MOLT token rose sharply and then fell about 75% with no product behind it.
- https://www.moltbook.com/post/13c58e8d-5774-4d6e-8b2c-64f2f82f56de

This is why the project has **no token**. Early users get non-transferable credits for the service itself.
